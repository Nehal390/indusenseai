## 🚀 Overview

**InduSense AI** is an AI-powered Industrial Product Intelligence platform designed to help businesses manage and understand large industrial product catalogs.

Industrial product data is often fragmented across CSV/XLSX files and can contain:

- Inconsistent product names
- Missing specifications
- Duplicate products
- Poor categorization
- Unstructured descriptions
- Difficult-to-search information

InduSense AI converts this raw catalog data into an intelligent product knowledge system that enables businesses to **understand, search, compare, recommend and manage industrial products more efficiently**.
